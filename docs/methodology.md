# AI Brand Visibility Analyzer Methodology

The **AI Brand Visibility Analyzer** is a public prototype by **Digital Platform 271** designed to demonstrate how a brand website can be evaluated across multiple layers of AI visibility and recommendation readiness.

## Purpose

The prototype is intended to help founders, marketers, D2C brands and digital teams understand that AI visibility is not a single metric. A brand can be discoverable and still be weak in authority, citation readiness or recommendation readiness.

The framework separates that problem into five layers:

1. **AI Presence**  
   Whether the brand has enough visible and accessible signals to be found and recognized.

2. **AI Understanding**  
   Whether the website clearly communicates what the brand is, what it offers, who it serves and how its products or services are structured.

3. **AI Authority**  
   Whether the brand provides credible trust and expertise signals that support evaluation.

4. **Citation Authority**  
   Whether the site contains clear, sourceable evidence and information that can be referenced or cited with confidence.

5. **Recommendation Readiness**  
   Whether the website provides enough decision-useful information, proof, differentiation and context for a user or AI system to evaluate why the brand may be suitable for a specific need.

## Prototype scoring

The hackathon version uses deterministic demo logic to produce repeatable outputs across the five layers. It can surface:

- an overall AI visibility score
- five layer scores
- the main bottleneck
- quick wins
- prioritized remediation actions

The scoring is designed to demonstrate the product workflow and decision model rather than claim live measurement of ChatGPT, Gemini or other AI platforms.

## What the prototype does not do

The current public hackathon prototype does **not**:

- crawl the submitted website in real time
- query external AI platforms
- measure live citations or recommendations
- guarantee that a brand will be cited or recommended by any AI system

These limitations are intentional and are kept explicit so the prototype remains transparent and reproducible.

## Research context

The framework is connected to Digital Platform 271's broader AI visibility research, including the **AI Visibility Benchmark 2026**, which evaluated 30 Indian D2C brands across 53 website signals and five visibility dimensions.
