# Judge Guide

## AI Brand Visibility Analyzer

This page gives hackathon reviewers a quick way to understand and evaluate the prototype.

### 1. The problem

Brands can be discoverable in search and still lack the evidence, clarity, authority, and decision-useful information needed for AI systems to cite or recommend them confidently.

### 2. The solution

The AI Brand Visibility Analyzer demonstrates a five-layer diagnostic model:

1. AI Presence
2. AI Understanding
3. AI Authority
4. Citation Authority
5. Recommendation Readiness

The prototype helps identify where a brand is weakest, why that weakness matters, and what should be fixed first.

### 3. What to try

Open the live prototype:

https://www.digitalplatform271.com/ai-brand-visibility-analyzer

Enter a brand website/domain and review:

- overall AI visibility score
- five framework scores
- primary bottleneck
- quick wins
- prioritized remediation actions

### 4. Prototype scope

This hackathon version uses deterministic scoring to demonstrate the product workflow and five-layer decision model. It does not crawl the submitted website or query external AI platforms in real time.

The public repository intentionally excludes production systems, credentials, customer data, payment logic, and private report-generation logic.

### 5. Why the framework matters

The project is designed around a practical distinction:

**Being found by AI is not the same as being trusted enough to be cited or recommended.**

That distinction is especially relevant for D2C and ecommerce brands where product discovery increasingly happens through conversational and generative interfaces.

### 6. Research connection

The framework is informed by Digital Platform 271's AI Visibility Benchmark 2026, which evaluated 30 Indian D2C brands across 53 website signals and five AI visibility dimensions.
